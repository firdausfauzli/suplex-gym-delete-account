Suplex Gym — Account and Data Deletion

Suplex Gym members can request deletion of their account and associated personal data, even if they do not have a self-service deletion option in the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Contact Suplex Gym through WhatsApp using the button below.
  2. Send the message: “I request deletion of my Suplex Gym account and associated personal data.”
  3. Include the mobile number or email address registered to your account so we can identify it. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you own the account. Do not send passwords or payment card details.

Request account deletion on WhatsApp

What data may be deleted

After verifying the request, Suplex Gym will delete or anonymise account information that is no longer needed, where deletion is permitted. This may include your name, mobile number, email address, membership information, and gym entry records associated with your account.

Data that may be retained

Some payment, transaction, security, or business records may need to be retained where required by law, for accounting, to resolve disputes, or to prevent fraud. Any retained data will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose and then deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

Processing time

We will review your request and contact you if we need additional information to verify it. Processing time may depend on verification and any legal or operational retention requirements.

This page is for Suplex Gym account and data deletion requests. Sending a request does not automatically confirm that deletion has been completed; Suplex Gym will review and process the request.
